Weather Satellite Imaging Receiver

RF · DSP · Ground Station

A DSP pipeline built in GNU Radio and CST Studio that filters, demodulates, synchronizes, decodes, and reconstructs METEOR M2-4 weather-satellite transmissions into Earth imagery. The physical ground station runs on an RTL-SDR with a hand-built V-dipole antenna, FIR filtering, and FM demodulation — achieving >90% successful image reconstruction across 5 continents.
